# Product marks

Validaira uses bundled brand marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) where an exact product mark is available. The copied SVGs are from Simple Icons 16.34.0 and are distributed under CC0; see [SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md](SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md) and [SIMPLE-ICONS-DISCLAIMER.md](SIMPLE-ICONS-DISCLAIMER.md).

Marks remain associated with their respective owners and may be trademarks. For products without an exact Simple Icons entry, the app uses the product's official website favicon. Generic Validaira industry packs use a relevant category symbol rather than an invented vendor logo.
